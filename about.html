Meet Lauryn

Writing theology with its feet on the floor for everyday faithfulness in real life.

I'm Lauryn Malone—a Christian writer, curriculum developer, worship leader, and everyday mom. I live in Alabama with my husband and our three boys, which means most of my quiet moments are... not quiet at all.

By day, I write for a Christian homeschool curriculum company, creating biblically grounded lessons that help families engage Scripture thoughtfully and faithfully.

In the local church, I've spent years leading worship, teaching Bible studies, planning events, and encouraging women to build their lives around the truth of God's Word.

Those experiences have shaped one steady conviction: worship isn't confined to music or church services. It's daily surrender. Daily obedience. Daily trust in Christ when life feels ordinary, overwhelming, beautiful, or broken.

My writing explores worship as a lifestyle, identity anchored in Christ, and faith that remains steady in kitchens, car rides, laundry rooms, and everyday moments.

Whether through books, Bible studies, blog posts, or conversations over coffee, my prayer is simple: that people would see Jesus more clearly and learn to worship Him in every room they walk through.
